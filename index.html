ABOUT DIVYASREE

Packaging made to your specification.

Established in 2009 under the leadership of A. Sridhar, Divyasree Woven Sacks focuses on customized woven-sack requirements rather than one-size-fits-all packaging. Customers can discuss the required dimensions, denier, construction, printing and lamination based on their application.

Our goal is simple: dependable packaging, practical customization and consistent service for businesses that need woven sacks in volume.

Materials & specifications

  • HDPE, PP and BOPP options
  • Denier range from 500 to 1500
  • Required bag dimensions and construction
  • Printing and branding requirements
  • Lamination requirements
